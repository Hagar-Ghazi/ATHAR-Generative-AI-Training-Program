# Week 2 Lab: Generative AI in Your Department

**Assignment:** Choose one real problem or routine task in your own department and write a two-page brief explaining whether Generative AI could help, what might go wrong, and what information must stay private. See [Week 2 Lab Deliverable.pdf](Week%202%20Lab%20Deliverable.pdf).

## Submission: AI-Powered Research Reproducibility Assistant

**Department:** Machine Learning and Information Retrieval, Faculty of Artificial Intelligence, Kafrelsheikh University

The brief proposes a Generative AI assistant that reads a project's research files (Python scripts, Jupyter notebooks, configuration files and training logs) and drafts a structured experiment report. The report covers datasets, model configuration, results, reproduction steps and missing information and every statement is traced to its source for the researcher to review.

| Section | Summary |
|---|---|
| 1. The Problem | Experiment details are scattered across files and personal notes, so past experiments are hard to reproduce or hand over. |
| 2. Why Generative AI Fits | It rebuilds documentation for past, untracked work which experiment-tracking tools such as MLflow and Weights & Biases cannot do. |
| 3. What Could Go Wrong | Hallucinated details, misleading comparisons between experiments, and leakage of confidential research each with mitigations. |
| 4. Data That Must Never Leave the Institution | Unpublished research, peer-review material, private code, restricted datasets, student information, credentials and more. |
